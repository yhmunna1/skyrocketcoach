The Challenge

What was getting in the way

  • No time to build and connect a funnel while serving clients full-time.
  • The tech stack pages, checkout, Zoom, automations felt like a full-time job on its own.
  • Previous freelancers left things half-finished, with weak communication and no ownership.
  • Missed reminders and broken links meant registrations that never showed up.

The Goal

What we set out to deliver

  • A reliable webinar funnel that runs start to finish without hands-on management.
  • Every registrant guided smoothly from sign-up to live room to offer.
  • Automations that follow up consistently, so no lead slips through.
  • One reliable team taking ownership of the build and keeping it running.
